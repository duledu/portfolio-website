A custom AgentFire platform for Sure Group, a Baltimore-based team behind more than $1B in closed sales — replacing a dated template with a bold, conversion-focused site built around clear paths for buyers, sellers, and agents joining the team.
The Challenge

A brand that needed a site as bold as its reputation.

Sure Group had built a reputation as one of Maryland's most relentless real estate teams, but their website didn't reflect it. The design felt generic, the buyer and seller journeys were buried, and the site did little to support a growing roster of agents.

The challenge was to build something that matched the energy of the "Confidence Guaranteed" brand — while integrating a complex MLS/IDX property search and giving buyers, sellers, and prospective agents a clear path from the very first screen.

The Solution

Confidence guaranteed. Design to match.

I designed and built the entire site from scratch — starting with a custom design system, then developing on AgentFire for performance and flexibility. Every path, from buyer to seller to agent recruiting, was purpose-built.

  • Custom property search with map-based IDX/MLS integration
  • Bold, overlapping hero imagery with a signature diagonal grid
  • Dedicated buyer, seller, and "join the team" pathways
  • Agent and team profile pages with CMS-editable bios
  • Sub-100ms server response time on all pages
  • 98 PageSpeed score on desktop and 94 on mobile
Design Direction

Bold, confident, unmistakably Sure Group.

The palette pairs deep charcoal with a single plum accent, echoing the brand's "Confidence Guaranteed" promise. Bold, oversized headlines carry the same energy as the team itself, while a diagonal image grid gives the homepage rhythm and movement.

Generous whitespace and large photography let the team and their listings speak. Subtle scroll-based animations create a sense of depth without distraction.

Development

Clean code. Lasting performance.

Built on a fully custom AgentFire theme, with hand-tuned templates for near-instant initial loads. Listing data is fetched server-side from the IDX/MLS feed and cached at the edge for near-instant page loads.
