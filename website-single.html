A bespoke luxury real estate platform that elevated the Meridian brand from outdated WordPress template to a polished, conversion-optimized digital presence — resulting in a 60% increase in qualified leads within 90 days of launch.
The Challenge

A brand demanding more than a template could offer.

Meridian Luxury Properties was operating on a stock real estate theme that undermined the premium nature of their listings. The old site loaded slowly, felt generic, and failed to communicate the level of service their clients expected. Leads were bouncing before they ever saw a property.

The challenge was to build something that felt as exclusive as the properties themselves — while integrating a complex MLS/IDX property search and remaining editable by their in-house team without developer support.

The Solution

Custom from the ground up. No shortcuts.

I designed and built the entire site from scratch — starting with a custom design system in Figma, then developing in Next.js for performance and flexibility. Every element, from property card layouts to search filters, was purpose-built.

  • Custom property search with map-based IDX integration
  • Animated hero with video background and noise overlay
  • Agent profile system with CMS-editable bios
  • Neighborhood guide pages with local statistics
  • Sub-100ms server response time on all pages
  • 99 PageSpeed score on desktop and 95 on mobile
Design Direction

Quiet luxury. Confident space.

The palette is built on deep charcoal, warm cream, and a single gold accent — echoing materials found in the properties themselves. Typography pairs a refined serif display face with a clean geometric sans for body copy.

Generous whitespace and large imagery let the properties speak. Subtle scroll-based animations create a sense of depth without distraction.

Development

Clean code. Lasting performance.

Built in Next.js 14 with the App Router, using React Server Components for fast initial loads. Property data is fetched server-side from the IDX API and cached at the edge for near-instant page loads.
