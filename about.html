The Developer

I'm Dusan "Drake" Stevanovic — a senior web designer and front-end developer with 11+ years of experience and 500+ websites delivered for clients across the US and Europe. Along the way I also served as CTO for a media company, which shapes how I think about technology beyond just the front end.

My focus sits at the intersection of design and hands-on front-end implementation — most of it inside the US real estate market, building on WordPress and AgentFire for agents, teams, and brokerages. I work directly with clients, manage complex revisions, and run several projects at once without losing sight of the details.

Day to day that means WordPress, Elementor, and custom HTML / CSS / SCSS / JavaScript — built beyond what a page builder can do on its own — backed by AI-assisted workflows that keep delivery fast without cutting corners.

How I Think

Principles I build by.

01

Craft over speed

I'd rather take the time to get it right than ship something I'm not proud of. Fast and cheap projects aren't what I do — precise, considered work is.

02

Design earns its place

Every design decision should serve a purpose: communicate, guide, convert. Decoration without function is just noise.

03

Code is the product

Clean, semantic, maintainable code isn't optional — it's the foundation everything else depends on. Messy code creates messy outcomes.

04

Honest partnership

I'll tell you when an idea won't work. I'll push back when needed. Good projects come from honest collaboration, not just order-taking.
