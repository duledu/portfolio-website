How I Think

Principles I build by.

01

Craft over speed

I'd rather take the time to get it right than ship something I'm not proud of. Fast and cheap projects aren't what I do — precise, considered work is.

02

Design earns its place

Every design decision should serve a purpose: communicate, guide, convert. Decoration without function is just noise.

03

Code is the product

Clean, semantic, maintainable code isn't optional — it's the foundation everything else depends on. Messy code creates messy outcomes.

04

Honest partnership

I'll tell you when an idea won't work. I'll push back when needed. Good projects come from honest collaboration, not just order-taking.
