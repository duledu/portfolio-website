The Developer

I'm Dušan "Drake" Stevanović, senior web designer and front-end developer, and the person behind AXIOM. I've spent 18+ years in this industry, the last 11+ of them working at senior level, most of it at the intersection of design and hands-on front-end work. Along the way I also served as CTO for a media company, which still shapes how I think about technology beyond the front end.

AXIOM is my own name for this: the identity I use to bring together design, front-end development, and the way I think about building things. It's not a studio or a company, and the work you'll see here belongs to my career, not to AXIOM as a business. AXIOM is simply how I'm choosing to present it, and an idea I expect to keep shaping over time.

My focus sits at the intersection of design and hands-on front-end implementation, most of it inside the US real estate market, building on WordPress and AgentFire for agents, teams, and brokerages. I work directly with clients, manage complex revisions, and run several projects at once without losing sight of the details.

For most of my career, I haven't needed a portfolio like this. Work has generally come through people I'd already worked with: recommendations, referrals, the next project following naturally from the last one.

I'm putting this together now because I want to change that, on purpose. I'm looking to work with new people, on new problems, in environments I haven't been part of before. There's still plenty I want to learn, and I'd like my experience to be useful to the people I work with along the way, sharing it where it helps, and learning from them in return.

Day to day that means WordPress, Elementor, and custom HTML / CSS / SCSS / JavaScript, built beyond what a page builder can do on its own, backed by AI-assisted workflows that keep delivery fast without cutting corners.

How I Think

Principles I build by.

01

Craft over speed

I'd rather take the time to get it right than ship something I'm not proud of. Fast and cheap projects aren't what I do; precise, considered work is.

02

Design earns its place

Every design decision should serve a purpose: communicate, guide, convert. Decoration without function is just noise.

03

Code is the product

Clean, semantic, maintainable code isn't optional; it's the foundation everything else depends on. Messy code creates messy outcomes.

04

Honest partnership

I'll tell you when an idea won't work. I'll push back when needed. Good projects come from honest collaboration, not just order-taking.

How I Work Today

The tools change. The standards don't.

I've been doing this long enough to have lived through a few real shifts in how websites get designed and built. AI-assisted tools are the current one, and I've built them into how I work rather than working around them.

Day to day that means tools like VS Code, Claude, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Git and GitHub, browser developer tools, and standard design software, used for research, iteration, implementation, debugging, and QA. This site was built the same way.

What doesn't change is what I'm responsible for: the direction, the design decisions, the technical calls, and making sure everything I ship actually holds up. The tools help me move faster. They don't make the decisions.
