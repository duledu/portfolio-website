What I Do

Design experience,
backed by technical depth.

Six areas I bring to a team, built on 18+ years of hands-on production work, from visual design through front-end implementation to technical leadership.

01

Web Design & UX/UI

Visual design, UX/UI, and responsive systems built around a clear hierarchy and conversion in mind: landing pages, marketing sites, and product interfaces, backed by reusable design systems rather than one-off pages.

Focus Areas

    Visual Design UX/UI Responsive Design Landing Pages Design Systems Conversion-Focused Design
02

Front-End Development

Semantic HTML, SCSS/Sass architecture, and clean JavaScript and jQuery, kept lean and fast. Whether it's a design I've created or one handed off from another designer, I turn it into precise, performant code that holds up across every device.

Focus Areas

    HTML SCSS / Sass CSS JavaScript jQuery Bootstrap
03

WordPress & Web Platforms

Custom WordPress websites built with Elementor and Elementor Pro, taken well beyond a stock page-builder layout with custom theme work where it's needed, plus six years deep in AgentFire's real estate platform, from CMS architecture to third-party integrations and optimization.

Focus Areas

    WordPress Elementor AgentFire CMS Architecture Theme Customization Third-Party Integrations
04

Product & Technical Thinking

Beyond client websites, I've designed and helped build SaaS products: product UX, workflow design, payment flows, and technical planning alongside engineering. Former CTO experience means I think about architecture and delivery, not just the interface.

Focus Areas

    SaaS Interfaces Product UX Technical Planning Stripe Vercel Engineering Collaboration
05

AI-Assisted Workflow

Modern AI tools built into day-to-day work: research, ideation, development, QA, and debugging, to move faster without cutting corners on quality or code I'd stand behind. Direction, design judgment, and final quality control stay mine. More on how that works on About.

Focus Areas

    AI-Assisted Design AI-Assisted Development Research & Ideation AI-Assisted QA Workflow Automation
06

Leadership & Collaboration

Technical leadership from a former CTO role, plus years of direct client, PM, and developer collaboration inside Jira- and Slack-based workflows, running multiple projects at once without losing sight of the details.

Focus Areas

    Technical Leadership Stakeholder Management Client Communication Cross-Functional Collaboration Jira Slack
